06 Systems

Two Dashboards
Built From Scratch

When the tools available didn't fit my workflow, I replaced them. I built a custom CRM to handle lead capture, pipeline tracking, and automated quote delivery. A Content Dashboard centralises every asset, caption, and content framework I produce.

Why This Exists
Most marketing teams run on Notion docs, Google Drive folders, and WhatsApp threads. These tools were built specifically for how Supersub works: with the workflows, terminology, and data structure that actually fits the business. That's the difference between using software and building it.
01. CRM & Sales Dashboard
Supersub CRM · quotation-and-sales-dashboard.vercel.app · Click to view full size ↗
01.
Multi-Channel Lead Capture
Automatically captures leads from website forms and Meta/Facebook Ads. Each lead is tagged by source, scored, and enters the pipeline in real-time.
02.
Lead Scoring System
Leads are automatically scored based on intent signals. General enquiries score 15; specific product enquiries (e.g. "2.4m Portable Bathroom") score 35. Sales team prioritises accordingly.
03.
Quote Builder & Automated Email Delivery
Templates built per product line (SS-PB, etc). Generate a branded quote with product details, pricing, validity date and delivery location, sent directly to the customer's inbox with one click. Quote numbering auto-increments (SS-PB-2026-003).
04.
Invite-Only Authentication
Role-based access control: only team members invited by email can sign up and log in. Lead and pipeline data is protected from public access.
05.
Built in Under 1 Month
Designed, developed and deployed while managing all other Supersub marketing deliverables simultaneously. Replaced HubSpot, saving $800+/month in SaaS costs.
02. Content Dashboard

A centralised hub for every piece of content I produce for Supersub: carousels, videos, captions, scheduling, content frameworks, and ad strategy. No Google Drive folders, no scattered documents.
