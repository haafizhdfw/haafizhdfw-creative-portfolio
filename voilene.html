01 The Project

Instagram Feeds for a
Women's Fashion Brand

Voilène Etoile is a women's fashion brand designed to spread positive aura and bring new colors to every woman's body. The brand philosophy: comfort, beauty, and empowerment, for women of all sizes. I handled the visual identity and Instagram feeds design to help the brand launch with a cohesive, elegant aesthetic.

  • Platform

02 What I Did

My Scope of Work

I handled every Instagram visual for Voilène Etoile, from moodboard to photo retouch, to build a brand image that's elegant, soft, and consistent.

01.
Instagram Moodboard
Built the visual direction for Voilène Etoile's Instagram: a soft color palette (cream, sage, teal), elegant serif typography, and a grid aesthetic reflecting the brand philosophy: comfort and beauty for every body.
Graphic Design
02.
Feeds Instagram Design
Designed every Instagram feeds post, including the product launch announcement and product collection posts (LAUNCHED, ADELINE, ANNELISE, BLANCHE, BLISSE, BIJOU), with consistent, on-brand typography overlays on every post.
Graphic Design
03.
Photo Retouch
Retouched all product photography: skin correction, consistent color grading, and crop framing to make sure every photo looked cohesive and high-quality on the Instagram feed.
Graphic Design

Selected Output

A collection of Instagram feed designs produced for Voilène Etoile, from the launch announcement to the product collection series.

Final Output
